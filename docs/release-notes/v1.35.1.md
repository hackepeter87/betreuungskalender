# v1.35.1

`v1.35.1` makes the owner-facing member and privacy workflows easier to scan
and operate without changing their existing server-side rules.

## Member administration

- Existing members are shown before the invitation action.
- Invitation creation opens in a focused responsive dialog instead of sharing
  one dense settings panel with the member list.
- Role and care-party scope choices are visually separated and reviewed before
  an invitation or access change is applied.
- Member rows keep identity, access summary, and actions readable on tablet and
  desktop widths.

## Privacy workflow

- The settings page provides one compact entry into the privacy workflow.
- Subject selection, affected data, warnings, blockers, confirmation, and the
  final result stay inside one guided dialog.
- The workflow still presents technical consequences only; the operator makes
  the underlying legal decision outside the application.

## Compatibility

- No API, database schema, authentication, authorization, backup, or transfer
  contract changes are included.
- SQLite and PostgreSQL installations remain compatible.

The Helm chart is `0.16.1` with application version `1.35.1`. The release is
published first to `testing`. Promotion to `production` or `latest` requires a
separate explicit approval.
